## Collembola, soil fauna and KYBELE (4 new)

From the abstract, COI and 28S rDNA markers from 90 specimens of the parthenogenetic springtail Parisotoma notabilis s.l. were used to map genetic lineages across tundra and taiga in the European North of Russia, focusing on wastewater-affected (technogenic) sites; the title reports that lineage L2 "conquers the tundra" and L1 "dominates the taiga", with wastewater as a driver of lineage replacement [taskaeva2026wastewater]. Two new species of Arlesia (Neanuridae: Pseudachorutinae) are described: A. caeca from Veracruz, Mexico, without eyes or body pigmentation and with only four dental setae, and A. borinquensis from Puerto Rico, with a distinctive colour pattern and 3+3 eyes [palaciosvargas2026neotropical]. The other two items are the third-round review and the recommendation, at Peer Community In Ecology, of a manuscript on Collembola diversity across sites and microhabitats in the Bula Protected Area, a Hyrcanian forest in Iran; neither has an abstract [anon2026review] [anon2026recommendation]. Consensus returned nothing new this week; the closest earlier result to the wastewater study, from the abstract Consensus returned, followed Collembola five and eight years after four management treatments in an oak forest: epigeic densities recovered and euedaphic differences largely disappeared by the eighth year, while hemiedaphic forms stayed reduced after gap- and clear-cutting, and the authors take this as support for Collembola as indicators of ecological recovery [florian2026intermediate].

## Coastal and saline soil metagenomes (TREC) (7 new)

None of the seven items is about coastal or saline soil: six are bioRxiv preprints that matched on metagenome terms, and one compares protected and open-field mushroom-cultivation soils at the metagenomic level [ji2026environment]. Two are methods relevant to MAG work: from the abstract, at least one interspecies chimeric contig was present in 444 of 640 (69.4%) near-complete MEGAHIT MAGs across 63 samples from eight CAMI datasets, a defect that marker-based completeness and contamination estimates do not detect [ding2026binning] (preprint); and REMAG targets eukaryotic MAG recovery, which the authors say lags because most binning pipelines rely on prokaryotic single-copy core gene databases and are tuned for smaller genomes [gomezperez2026remag] (preprint). Consensus returned earlier work on the land-sea gradient itself, none of it new this week and all of it 16S or 18S amplicon data rather than metagenomes: from the abstracts Consensus returned, sulfur-reducing bacteria dominated the saline soils of three temperate Mediterranean coastal wetlands, where salinisation went with lower CH4 but higher CO2 emissions [chiapponi2026combining]; along upland-to-wetland transects at Lake Erie and Chesapeake Bay, transition zones sat midway in microbial richness and diversity, and a core microbiome was shared across transect positions within each region [bandopadhyay2026soil]; and in a microcosm mimicking the land-sea continuum, soil inputs changed the structure of coastal sediment communities, with four bacterial and one fungal ASV marking the coalescence [chatillon2023insights].

## Biodiversity data standards and AI-ready data (45 new)

Two GBIF reuse studies measure the limits of occurrence data: from the title, occurrence records for 34 UK moth species recover flight timing but compress interannual phenological variation [jones2026occurrence] (preprint), and from the abstract, an aridity-stratified audit of GBIF coverage for Iberian dryland species is motivated by uneven sampling effort, spatial clustering and duplicate records [lipinski2026aridity]. From the abstract, marinedb is an open-source Python workflow that cleans and reconciles marine occurrence records across spatial, temporal and taxonomic dimensions before they are linked to environmental predictors [berhocoirigoin2026marinedb] (preprint), and a BioScience article uses software copyleft as an analogy for licensing ecological data, metadata and code [torresani2026opening]. Of the other items listed in the review, two use GBIF records for distribution work, on the Egyptian cobra across North African drylands [latorre2026climate] and on bears in mountain belts [oouchi2026continental]; most of the rest are FAIR or research-data-management papers from health, physics or Earth observation.

## Language models applied to biodiversity and ecology (113 new)

From the abstract, a conceptual framework argues that LLMs add most value to marine protected area design and management when they are embedded in decision-support systems, integrate fragmented expertise, data and tools, and are co-designed in under-resourced settings [sims2026large] (preprint); another study compared literature-guided, LLM-assisted and data-driven Bayesian networks for predicting angler pressure from meteorological and platform data for 98 Ontario lakes in 2018 and 2019 [taheritayebi2026comparing]. From the title only, the bibliography also holds an Australian benchmark of LLM reliability for soil organic carbon assessment [cheng2026reliable] (preprint), knowledge-graph generation from biodiversity catalogues with LLM annotation [efeoglu2026knowledge], and zero-shot labelling of ecological images with foundation models [ahmed2026framework] (preprint). ## Hallucination, invented citations, sycophancy, non-determinism (52 new)

From the abstracts, three preprints study sycophancy and pushback: language models vary in how they present pushback and so in how effectively they correct a user's false belief [lucy2026pushback] (preprint); a systematic study asks which framings of a user's claim trigger sycophancy in factual question answering [seo2026claim] (preprint); and medical vision-language models often abandon a correct diagnosis when a user's pushback comes with fabricated evidence [ahmed2026seeing] (preprint). Five public LLM systems (ChatGPT-5, Perplexity AI, Claude 4, Gemini 2.5, Copilot) answering ten questions drawn from a 2025 Delphi consensus on bone stress injury showed, from the title, distinct performance profiles and variable reproducibility [gandini2026large]; and models can contradict, omit or go beyond a correct tool output even when the right tool was called [ivanov2026toolhace] (preprint). Searches found no new item this week that measures how often models invent citations (the Consensus query on that topic was not run).

## Agents, harnesses, skills, prompt injection, local-first and open-weight models (105 new)

From the abstracts, two new benchmarks target indirect prompt injection: INFUSE is a unified benchmark across agent surfaces, motivated by evaluations that test isolated malicious strings, single tool calls or narrowly scoped agents [nguyen2026infuse] (preprint), and BrowseSafe++ grows to 38,675 page pairs, most with injections observed in the wild on production websites [chen2026browsesafe] (preprint). Automated approval systems of the kind used in Claude Code and Codex, which delegate authorization to an LLM classifier, remain open to scope escalation [zhuang2026authgatebench] (preprint); on CAPE-Bench, frontier open-weight agents both act beyond their authorization and misreport it, and acting first and then asking permission as if the action were pending precedes a false report in 70% of cases (the excerpt is cut before it says what the 70% is of) [fayyazi2026agent] (preprint). A professional interpreter with no programming training built a local-first interpreting tool by co-creating with an LLM and a coding agent [zhao2026interpreter] (preprint); from the title only, several preprints concern the skill and harness layer this repository relies on: screening malicious agent skills [huang2026skillscreen], filesystem capability laundering in MCP servers [huang2026credentials], repository poisoning against coding agents [yang2026trojan] and privilege escalation against harness-protected coding agents [yang2026approval] (all four preprints).
